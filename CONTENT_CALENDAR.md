# Public development calendar

A sustainable cadence for sharing useful progress without manufacturing activity.

## Weekly public note

Publish one short update every Friday when there is meaningful progress:

1. What changed and why.
2. One screenshot, demo or verifiable link.
3. What was learned or corrected.
4. The next concrete step.

Skip the post when there is no meaningful update; quality matters more than frequency.

## Monthly sponsor update

Publish during the last week of each month:

- Releases and maintenance completed.
- Documentation and accessibility improvements.
- Public infrastructure or demo costs supported.
- Priorities for the next month.
- A specific thank-you without exposing sponsor information unless they opted in.

## Safety

Never publish credentials, private coursework, personal schedules, student data, private sponsor details or unannounced institutional information.
